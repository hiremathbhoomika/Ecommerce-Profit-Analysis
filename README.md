# Ecommerce-Profit-Analysis

E-commerce sales and profitability analysis using Python, Pandas and data visualization.

## Project Overview

This project analyzes e-commerce sales and profitability data to understand
why company profit is falling despite revenue growth.

The analysis focuses on sales performance, discounts, customer behavior,
delivery performance, product profitability, and profit margins.

## Business Problem

The company is experiencing unstable profitability even though revenue is
increasing. The objective of this project is to identify the key factors
affecting profit and provide data-driven business recommendations.

## Dataset

The project contains three datasets:

- `customers.csv` – customer details, city, age, and membership
- `orders.csv` – order, sales, discount, delivery, and payment information
- `products.csv` – product and pricing information

Dataset size:
- 800 customers
- 3,500 orders
- 200 products

## Key Findings

- Total revenue was approximately ₹43.98 lakh.
- Total profit was approximately ₹7.92 lakh.
- Overall profit margin was approximately 19.48%.
- Higher discounts were associated with lower profit margins.
- 0% discount orders had a higher margin than heavily discounted orders.
- Repeat customers generated a major share of total revenue and profit.
- Late deliveries were common, but the analysis did not show a lower profit
  margin for late orders.
